# Gorge Kite

A browser kiteboarding game with Columbia Gorge west-wind energy. Fly the kite, edge the board, send jumps and kiteloops, and try not to get yoinked.

Play it: **https://jeffolfert.github.io/gorge-kite/**

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steer kite left / right | ← → or A D | Left stick (horizontal) |
| Kite high / power zone | ↑ ↓ or W S | Left stick (vertical) |
| Edge the board | hold ↓ or S | Pull stick down or hold EDGE |
| Jump / pop | Space | POP |
| Grab in the air | Shift or G | GRAB |
| Start / restart | Space, click, or tap | tap |

## Scoring

- **Distance** — ride downstream
- **Airtime** — stay up
- **Jump** — leave the water loaded
- **Grab** — hold GRAB while airborne
- **Kiteloop** — fly the kite in a full circle in the air
- **Handlepass** — spin a full 360 while grabbing
- Combos stack if you land clean and send again quickly
- Best run is saved in the browser

Wind comes and goes. Overpowered and not edging = yoink. No power = stall. Wipeouts are cheap — one button and you’re back on the water.
